# Playzy

Free TikTok LIVE interactive games for your PC. Viewers send gifts, likes and follows, and things happen in your game: a tank in GTA V, creepers in Minecraft, a key press in anything else.

No event limits, no watermark, no subscription, no account.

**[Download PlayzySetup.exe](https://github.com/faisalrasheed442/playzy-public/releases/latest)** (Windows 10 and 11)

After you install it, Playzy keeps itself up to date.

## Works with

- GTA V Story Mode: 41 effects and 8 challenges (Chiliad Race, Hiking, Can You Kill Me?, Last Standing, Taxi, Mega Ramp, Speed Bomb). Needs ScriptHookV and ScriptHookVDotNet; Playzy installs its own script.
- Minecraft Java, through RCON.
- Any PC game, through keyboard and mouse.
- Other mods, through a webhook or the Playzy SDK.

The overlay goes into TikTok LIVE Studio or OBS, tall (1080 × 1920) or wide (1920 × 1080).

Only use Playzy with GTA V Story Mode, never GTA Online.
