A Rose gives you a speed boost in GTA V. A Doughnut drops a creeper next to you in Minecraft. 300 likes and you jump, in any PC game. Playzy reads your TikTok LIVE and runs it in your game, with an overlay that shows viewers what each gift does.

Gifts in, game actions out

GTA V Story Mode

56 effects, from tanks and jets to chimps, meteors and a black hole, and 20 challenges with a win/loss score, from the Chiliad Race to Zombie Apocalypse, a Boss Fight and Battle Royale. Viewers can join three of them from the chat with !join.

Minecraft Java

Mobs, TNT, lightning, potion effects, items, weather or any server command, over RCON, on your own server or a rented one.

Any PC game

Gifts press keys, hold them, type, click and turn the camera. Keys only go to your game's window, never to your browser.

An overlay for LIVE Studio

One link, portrait or landscape: the gift menu, alerts with combos, a like goal, top gifters and the game HUD. OBS works too.

Gift spinner and voice

A wheel that picks a random action for a gift or !spin, and lines spoken with the voices Windows already has.

Test it before you go live

Fake viewers send gifts, likes and chat through your preset, exactly like real ones, so you know it works first.

Also in the box: gift actions made for the GTA V challenges (a zombie horde, healing the boss, adding or stealing time), challenges that only take the gifts that fit them and turn the rest into ones that do, viewers who join Battle Royale, Viewer Racers and Team War by typing !join, !red or !blue, viewers' names over the enemies they send, coin tiers for gifts without a rule, combos counted once or per gift with a limit, cooldowns per rule and per viewer, chat commands, presets you can export and share, a webhook for any URL, and a WebSocket SDK for mods of other games. A gift that arrives during a cooldown waits its turn; free likes and chat are skipped instead.
