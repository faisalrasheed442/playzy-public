What Playzy is

Playzy is a desktop app for Windows made by Faisal Malik at zylio. It reads gifts, likes, follows, shares and chat from your TikTok LIVE and turns them into actions in your PC games. It runs on your computer. zylio runs no server for it: there is no Playzy account, cloud or backend.

Every connection Playzy makes

  • Your TikTok LIVE. When you connect, Playzy reads your LIVE's public events (gifts, likes, follows, shares, joins, chat and the viewer count) with TikTokLive, an open-source library. It connects to TikTok's servers the way the TikTok website does, using only the TikTok name you typed in Settings. Playzy never asks for your TikTok password and never logs in to your account. TikTok's own privacy policy applies to your LIVE.
  • The Euler Stream signing service. TikTok requires each connection to be signed. TikTokLive asks the Euler Stream signing service to do that, so your TikTok name, the LIVE Playzy connects to, and your IP address reach Euler Stream every time Playzy connects. If you add a Sign API key in Settings, that key is sent to Euler Stream too. Euler Stream's own terms and privacy policy apply to what it receives.
  • Gift pictures and profile pictures. The overlay shows gift pictures and viewers' profile pictures, which load from TikTok's image servers into TikTok LIVE Studio or OBS. The Playzy window itself shows initials and never loads them.
  • Checking for updates. The installed app asks GitHub (api.github.com/repos/faisalrasheed442/playzy-public/releases/latest, or the releases feed on github.com when the API is busy) whether a newer version exists, a few seconds after it starts, and when you click Check for updates. The request carries no cookies and no identifier; its User-Agent says only Playzy/<version> and the releases address. GitHub sees your IP address, as it does for any visit, under GitHub's privacy statement. Nothing is downloaded until you click Download & install.
  • Minecraft, if you set it up. Playzy sends commands to the Minecraft server address you type on the Games page, over RCON, with the password you typed there. RCON is not encrypted, so for a server on the internet, use a password you use nowhere else.
  • Webhooks, if you add them. A rule with a webhook calls only the address you typed in that rule, with the details you chose to fill in (such as the viewer's name and the gift).

What Playzy opens on your own PC

  • The overlay and the Mod SDK are served on 127.0.0.1 (port 8790), so only programs on your own PC can open them. Requests a browser marks as coming from another website are refused.
  • The GTA V script listens inside the game on 127.0.0.1 (port 8766, or the next free one if another mod holds it). Only Playzy on the same PC talks to it.
  • Keyboard and mouse actions stay on your PC. They are only sent while the game window you chose is in front.

What Playzy never does

  • Send usage statistics, crash reports, analytics or any identifier to zylio or anyone else.
  • Ask for, store or send your TikTok password.
  • Upload your presets, settings or logs anywhere.
  • Show ads, or share or sell any information with anyone.

What stays on your computer

Playzy keeps its settings (including your TikTok name and Sign API key), presets, the gift list it learns from your LIVE, and its logs in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Playzy. The GTA V script keeps its settings, score and log in your GTA V scripts folder. Only you can see them. Logs never leave your computer unless you send one to us yourself.

Text to speech uses the voices built into Windows and runs on your PC. Nothing you have read out is sent anywhere.

Contacting us

If you email us for support, we use your message only to answer it. If you share a log to show a problem, it is deleted once the problem is fixed. We will never ask for your TikTok password.
